## 10.2 Environmental Performance and Climate Strategy

### Carbon Footprint Profile

**Current Emissions (2023–2024):**
- Scope 1 (Direct): 45-50 million tCO2e annually
  * Thermal power: 35-38 MtCO2e
  * Port operations: 2-3 MtCO2e
  * Other (fuel consumption): 5-7 MtCO2e
- Scope 2 (Indirect): 5-8 million tCO2e
- Scope 3 (Downstream): Not comprehensively measured

**Emissions Intensity:**
- Per rupee revenue: 220-240 tCO2e per ₹ crore revenue
- Per MW renewable: 25-30 gCO2e/kWh (excluding thermal)
- Thermal power fleet: 850-900 gCO2e/kWh (industry average: 850gCO2e/kWh)

### Net Zero Roadmap (2035–2050)

**Near-term Targets (2030):**
- Renewable capacity: 25+ GW (vs. 11 GW current)
- Coal exit: Phase-out initiation; thermal capacity reduction
- Emissions reduction: 40% reduction vs. 2022 baseline
- Energy efficiency: 25% improvement in specific energy consumption

**Medium-term Targets (2040):**
- Renewable dominance: 40+ GW capacity (vs. 10 GW thermal)
- Net zero (Scope 1+2): Carbon neutrality achieved (offset mechanisms)
- Hydrogen transition: 20% of energy portfolio from green hydrogen
- Electrification: Port and utility operations 80%+ electric

**Long-term Targets (2050):**
- Net zero (including Scope 3): Full value chain decarbonization
- Circular economy: 90%+ waste recycling, zero-waste operations
- Biodiversity: Restoration of 50,000+ hectares

### Environmental Initiatives and Programs

**Renewable Energy Expansion:**
- Capacity target: 25+ GW by 2030 (from 11 GW currently)
- Capex requirement: ₹15,000-18,000 crore over 2025–2030
- Technology focus: Solar (60%), Wind (30%), Emerging (10%)
- Impact: Elimination of 50-60 MtCO2e emissions annually

**Thermal Power Phase-Out:**
- Coal plant utilization: Gradual reduction beginning 2025
- Phase-out timeline: 50% capacity retired by 2040; 100% by 2050
- Transition support: Worker reskilling, community development programs
- Financial provision: Reserves being created for transition costs

**Port Operations Sustainability:**
- Electric equipment: 30% of port equipment electric/hybrid by 2025; 80% by 2035
- Hydrogen vehicles: Pilot projects initiated; scale-up planned 2025–2027
- Water conservation: Rainwater harvesting; desalination projects
- Waste management: 90% waste recycling target by 2030

**Green Hydrogen Initiative:**
- Pilot project: 5 MW pilot plant operational by 2026
- Commercial scale-up: 100+ MW by 2030
- Industrial partnerships: Collaborations with steel, refining, chemicals sectors
- Investment: ₹2,000-3,000 crore capex planned

## CHAPTER TAKEAWAYS

**ESG Positioning:** Currently in bottom quartile for Indian infrastructure peer group (35th percentile). Crisis accelerated governance improvements; environmental and social programs established. Opportunity for significant improvement through coordinated 2025–2030 initiative.

**Environmental Strategy:** Net zero by 2050 roadmap established; renewable capacity expansion central to emissions reduction. Thermal phase-out underway; green hydrogen emerging opportunity. Investment of ₹15,000–18,000 crore in renewable expansion required.

**Social Programs:** Substantial CSR investments (₹300–400 crore annually); 200,000+ direct employment, 500,000+ indirect. Community satisfaction and land acquisition management critical vulnerabilities. Labor relations stable; talent retention challenges emerging post-crisis.

**Governance Improvements:** Board independence increased to 50%; related-party governance strengthened. Executive compensation linked to ESG performance. Risk management infrastructure enhanced. Continued improvement required for top-quartile positioning.

**Sustainability Reporting:** Integrated reporting framework in place; GRI alignment progressing. TCFD and SASB disclosures emerging. Science-based targets validation pending 2025. Certification pathway clear for 2030 leadership.

**SDG Alignment:** Strong alignment with SDGs 7 (Energy), 8 (Work), 9 (Infrastructure), 13 (Climate). Moderate alignment with SDGs 11 (Cities), 12 (Production). Opportunity for enhanced community impact programs.

**Strategic Roadmap:** Three-phase ESG transformation (Foundation 2025–2027, Acceleration 2027–2035, Leadership 2035–2050) with ₹85,000–100,000 crore investment targeting ESG score improvement to 70+ by 2035.
