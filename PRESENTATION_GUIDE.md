### Slide 9: Gold Investment

- Low equity correlation
- Recommended allocation: 5-10%
**Visual Suggestion:** Gold CAGR trend chart, portfolio allocation pie

### Slide 10: Fixed Deposits (FDs)
**Content:**
- Guaranteed returns
- Current rates: 6-7% p.a.
- Insurance: ₹5 Lakh per bank (DICGC)
- TDS on interest above threshold
- Optimal tenure: 3-5 years
- Best for: Capital preservation
**Chart Suggestion:** FD rates comparison across banks

### Slide 11: PPF Overview
**Content:**
- Government-backed scheme
- Interest rate: 7.1% p.a. (tax-free)
- Tenure: 15 years + extension
- Annual limit: ₹500-1.5 Lakh
- EEE status (tax-exempt)
- Perfect for: Retirement planning
**Visual Suggestion:** Compounding growth chart for ₹1.5 Lakh annual investment

### Slide 12: Real Estate Investment
**Content:**
- Tangible asset with utility
- Historical CAGR: 8-12%
- Rental yield: 3-5% p.a.
- Mortgage financing available
- Liquidity: 6-12 months
- Allocation: 20-30% recommended
**Chart Suggestion:** Real estate CAGR vs other asset classes

### Slide 13: Post Office Schemes

- 🟢 Senior Citizen Saving Scheme: 8.2% p.a.

- 🔵 NSC: 8% p.a.

- Safe, government-backed, tax benefits
**Visual Suggestion:** Scheme comparison table with color-coded options

### Slide 14: Insurance as Investment
**Content:**

- Coverage + Wealth creation
- Benefits: Protection, tax deductions
- Caution: High fees, long lock-in
**Visual Suggestion:** Insurance vs Pure Investment comparison

### Slide 15: Risk vs Return Matrix
**Content:**

- Factors: Time horizon, financial obligations
**Chart Suggestion:** 2D Risk vs Return scatter plot matrix

### Slide 16: Smart Diversification Strategy
**Portfolio Models:**
- Conservative (60+ years): 40% FD + 30% Gold + 20% Bonds + 10% MF
- Balanced (35-60 years): 40% MF + 25% Real Estate + 20% FD + 15% Gold
- Aggressive (20-35 years): 60% Equity MF + 20% Stocks + 10% Gold + 10% FD
- Rebalancing: Quarterly or annually
- SIP Strategy: Monthly investments
- Review: Quarterly monitoring
