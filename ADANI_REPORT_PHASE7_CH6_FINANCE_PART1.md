# CHAPTER 6: FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS (1990–2025)

## 6.1 Historical Revenue Analysis

### Revenue Evolution Timeline

**Phase 1 (1990–1998): Trading Foundation**
- Period Revenue Scale: ₹50-200 crore
- CAGR: 25-30% (high growth from small base)
- Driver: Commodities trading expansion
- Characteristics: Low capital intensity, cyclical margins

**Phase 2 (1998–2010): Port Expansion**
- Period Revenue Scale: ₹200 crore → ₹3,000 crore
- CAGR: 25-28%
- Driver: Port capacity expansion (25 MTPA → 105 MTPA)
- Characteristics: Transition to asset-heavy, capital-intensive model

**Phase 3 (2010–2015): Energy Entry**
- Period Revenue Scale: ₹3,000 crore → ₹20,000 crore
- CAGR: 35-40%
- Driver: Thermal power plants, coal mining, utilities entry
- Characteristics: Accelerated diversification, multiple sector expansion

**Phase 4 (2015–2020): Conglomerate Consolidation**
- Period Revenue Scale: ₹20,000 crore → ₹100,000 crore
- CAGR: 35-40%
- Driver: Renewable energy acceleration, airports, Wilmar acquisition
- Characteristics: Portfolio diversification, scale achievement

**Phase 5 (2020–2025): Renewable Acceleration + Crisis/Recovery**
- Period Revenue Scale: ₹100,000 crore → ₹200,000+ crore
- CAGR: 15-20% (including 2023 crisis impact)
- Driver: Renewable capacity expansion, ports growth, utilities
- Characteristics: Renewable focus, crisis impact, recovery trajectory

### Current Revenue Scale (2025)

**Estimated Consolidated Revenue: ₹200,000-220,000 crore ($24-27 billion)**

**Segment Breakdown:**
- Ports: ₹55,000-60,000 crore (28%)
- Renewable Energy: ₹45,000-50,000 crore (22%)
- Thermal Power: ₹40,000-45,000 crore (22%)
- Consumer/Wilmar: ₹25,000-27,000 crore (12%)
- Gas Distribution: ₹18,000-20,000 crore (10%)
- Electricity Distribution: ₹8,000-10,000 crore (4%)
- Airports: ₹12,000-15,000 crore (6%)

### Revenue Quality Assessment

**Positive Characteristics:**
- 85%+ revenue under long-term contracts (ports, renewable PPAs, utilities)
- Government off-taker concentration reduces counterparty risk
- Essential infrastructure characteristics support pricing power
- Regulatory revenue mechanisms provide inflation protection (utilities)

**Vulnerabilities:**
- Regulatory pricing controls limiting upside
- Commodity price exposure (ports volume, thermal coal costs)
- Concession renewal risk (though typically extended)
- Cyclical segments (Wilmar, airports during travel downturns)

**Assessment:** Revenue base increasingly resilient with 85%+ contracted revenue, though regulatory constraints and commodity cycles create volatility.

## 6.3 Profitability Deep Dive: Return Metrics

### Return on Equity (ROE) Analysis

**Historical ROE Evolution:**

| Period | ROE | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| 2015 | 12-14% | Below cost of equity due to leverage build |
| 2018 | 14-16% | Improving as assets monetize |
| 2021 | 15-17% | Pre-crisis normalization |
| 2023 | 12-14% | Crisis impact, market cap decline |
| 2025 | 13-15% | Recovery trajectory |

**ROE Decomposition:**
- Profit Margin: 6-8% of revenues
- Asset Turnover: 0.8-1.0x (capital-intensive)
- Financial Leverage: 2.5-3.0x (D/E ~0.8-1.0)
- ROE = 6-8% × 0.8-1.0 × 2.5-3.0 = 12-24% range

**Benchmarking:**
- Target ROE for infrastructure: 15-18%
- Peer comparison (Reliance): 15-18%
- Investment grade threshold: >12%
- Adani performance: At lower end of target range

**Interpretation:** ROE improvement remains constrained by leverage, capital intensity, and regulatory margin controls. Renewable energy growth improving mix, but overall ROE recovery requires either margin expansion or leverage reduction.

### Return on Invested Capital (ROCE)

**Historical ROCE:**

| Period | ROCE | Invested Capital | Spread vs. WACC |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2015 | 10-12% | ₹60,000 crore | Below WACC |
| 2018 | 12-14% | ₹90,000 crore | At WACC |
| 2021 | 13-15% | ₹120,000 crore | Above WACC |
| 2025 | 12-14% | ₹150,000 crore | At/Below WACC |

**ROCE Trend Analysis:**
- Growing capital base diluting returns
- Renewable energy capex expanding base with lower short-term returns
- Mature port assets generating 15-18% ROCE
- Thermal power assets declining (stranding risk)

**Strategic Implication:** ROCE trend suggests marginal project returns are declining as group deploys capital into expansion. This implies either capital deployment discipline must increase or portfolio management focus on higher-return opportunities.

## 6.8 DuPont Analysis: ROE Decomposition

### ROE Waterfall

```
ROE = Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage

2015: 14% = 6.5% × 0.9x × 2.4x
2020: 15% = 6.8% × 0.85x × 2.6x
2025: 13% = 6.2% × 0.78x × 2.8x

DRIVERS OF CHANGE (2015 → 2025):
├─ Profit Margin: -0.3% (margin compression from capex, leverage)
├─ Asset Turnover: -0.12x (capital intensity increasing)
└─ Financial Leverage: +0.4x (debt levels increasing)

INTERPRETATION:
└─ Declining profit margin and asset turnover offset by increasing
   leverage, resulting in flat-to-declining ROE trajectory.
   Improvement requires either margin expansion or asset productivity.
```
