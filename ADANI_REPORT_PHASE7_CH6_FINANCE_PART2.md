## 6.9 Market Capitalization and Crisis Impact (2023)

### Pre-Crisis Valuation

**Peak Valuation (2022):**

- Valuation Multiple: 30-35x EBITDA (premium to peers)

**Valuation Drivers:**

### Hindenburg Impact and Recovery

**Crisis Timeline:**
- **January 2023:** Hindenburg Research alleges governance violations, improper related-party transactions, fund manipulation
- **Peak Decline:** Market cap falls ₹3-4 trillion (~20-25% from peak)
- **Liquidity Crisis:** Credit spreads widen; refinancing challenges
- **2024–2025:** Market recovery and stabilization

**Valuation Recovery:**
- 2023 Trough: ₹14-15 trillion
- 2024 Mid-year: ₹16-17 trillion
- 2025 Current: ₹18-19 trillion (approaching pre-crisis)

### Current Valuation (2025)

**Market Capitalization: ₹18-19 trillion ($220-230 billion)**

**Valuation Multiples:**
- EV/EBITDA: 20-22x (vs. 25-30x pre-crisis)
- P/E: 30-35x (vs. 40-45x pre-crisis)
- P/B: 2.2-2.5x
- Dividend Yield: 0.5-1.0%

**Valuation Assessment:**
- Trading at modest discount to pre-crisis due to lingering governance concerns
- Multiple compression reflects investor caution
- Still premium to Indian infrastructure peers (15-18x EV/EBITDA)
- Global infrastructure peers: 15-20x

**Interpretation:** Market valuation remains healthy but structural discount to pre-crisis reflects governance concerns and execution risk premium. Full rerating likely requires 2-3 years of clean execution and governance improvement evidence.

## STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS

### Financial Management Priorities

**Immediate (2025–2026):**
- Covenant compliance: Debt/EBITDA reduction targets
- Refinancing: Manage debt maturity wall prudently
- Capex discipline: Select high-ROCE projects only
- Communication: Build investor confidence through transparent reporting

**Medium-term (2026–2028):**
- Deleveraging: Target D/E reduction to 0.75x
- Asset monetization: Consider non-core asset sales
- Dividend policy: Begin modest distributions as FCF improves
- Portfolio optimization: Divest low-ROCE assets

**Long-term (2028–2035):**
- Sustainable leverage: Maintain D/E at 0.60–0.70x
- Growth reinvestment: Deploy FCF into emerging opportunities
- Capital structure: Consider equity issuance for large acquisitions
- Shareholder returns: Progressive dividend and buyback policy
